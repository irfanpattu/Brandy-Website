— FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?

Here are some common questions about our services.

What is included in social media management?

The agreed scope may include account management, content creation, scheduling and engagement. We confirm platforms and monthly deliverables before starting.

Which platforms do you run ads on?

We can manage campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google Ads and other suitable platforms depending on your goals.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timelines depend on page count, content readiness and required features. We confirm the schedule before the project begins.

Is advertising spend included?

No. The performance marketing fee covers campaign management. Ad spend is paid separately to the advertising platform.

Do you guarantee advertising results?

No specific advertising result can be guaranteed. Performance depends on the offer, audience, budget, competition and website experience.
