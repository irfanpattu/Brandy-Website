Your trust is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, and how you can contact us with privacy-related questions.

01 / OVERVIEW

Introduction

BRANDY is a marketing agency providing digital marketing, social media management, performance marketing, and website development services.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, or communicate with us about our services.

Where applicable law requires consent, we will obtain it through an appropriate process.

02 / DATA COLLECTION

Information We Collect

When you contact BRANDY or request information about our services, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and phone number.
  • Your business or company name.
  • Details about your project or service requirements.
  • Any additional information included in your enquiry.

If analytics or similar technologies are enabled, technical information such as browser type, device information, and website usage data may also be processed.

03 / PURPOSE

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to BRANDY may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Understand your business requirements.
  • Provide information about our services.
  • Communicate about ongoing projects.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We aim to use personal information only for relevant purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

04 / COMMUNICATION

Contact Forms & Enquiries

When you contact BRANDY through our website, WhatsApp, email, or another communication channel, the information you provide may be processed to respond to your enquiry.

If our website redirects you to WhatsApp or opens an email draft, your communication will be handled through the relevant third-party service.

!

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details, confidential documents, or other sensitive information through general website enquiries.

05 / DISCLOSURE

Information Sharing

BRANDY does not intend to sell personal information collected through this website.

Information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to operate our website, communicate with you, deliver requested services, or comply with legal requirements.

We aim to limit such sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

06 / PROTECTION

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information received through our website and business communications.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07 / STORAGE

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage business relationships, provide services, and meet applicable legal or administrative requirements.

Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate and legally permitted.

08 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the privacy laws applicable to you, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Ask questions about how your information is processed.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

09 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

Third-Party Services & External Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms or use third-party services, including hosting, communication, analytics, or marketing tools.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies. BRANDY does not control the privacy practices of external websites and encourages visitors to review their policies.

10 / UPDATES

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, business practices, services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
